Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PodClipper Hub ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you connect and interact with our automated publishing application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Authentication & Token Data: When you connect your TikTok account via OAuth 2.0, we obtain access tokens, refresh tokens, and authorized basic scopes strictly required to publish video content on your behalf.
  • Media & Upload Data: Video files, descriptions, tags, sound metadata, and posting preferences provided by you for automated publication.
  • Log Information: Basic operational event logs, timestamps, and API response codes used exclusively for error handling and system health monitoring.

2. How We Use Collected Data

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Authenticate authorized creator accounts.
  • Upload, schedule, and publish video content directly to your TikTok account as directed by your automation parameters.
  • Ensure stability and troubleshoot API integration errors.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or distribute personal data or media files to third-party advertisers or data brokers. Data transmission is strictly confined to communicating directly with TikTok's official developer endpoints via secure HTTPS protocols.

4. Data Retention & Security

We maintain appropriate technical safeguards to protect access tokens and authentication credentials. OAuth access tokens are stored securely and can be revoked at any time directly through your TikTok account security settings.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or requests regarding your data and this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contact@podclipperhub.com
